---
name: Clinical safety boundary
description: Safety and product scope rule for all clinical guidance and future image features.
---

Clinical outputs must remain explainable decision support for a qualified clinician, never diagnosis, autonomous treatment selection, or a claim of device suitability.

**Why:** The platform influences prosthetic planning and medical-adjacent care, where input quality, individual anatomy, and approved manufacturer instructions require clinician review.

**How to apply:** Keep outputs qualified with review prompts; use generic, traceable rationale; and do not label generated image/model results as production-ready without a validated clinical and regulatory workflow.
